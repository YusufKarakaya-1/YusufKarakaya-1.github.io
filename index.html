About me

Mechanical engineering with a high-tech systems perspective.

I am a Mechanical Engineering MSc student at TU Delft, specializing in High-Tech Engineering.

My main interest is the design of precision machines: mechanical systems where stiffness, dynamics, accuracy, thermal behaviour and manufacturability have to be considered together.

My coursework and projects also span mechatronics, dynamics, optics and micro & nano engineering, giving me a multidisciplinary perspective on high-tech systems.

I enjoy taking an engineering problem from requirements and concept generation through CAD, analysis, prototyping and experimental validation.
